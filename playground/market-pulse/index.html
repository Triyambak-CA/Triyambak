The market, where I work

A 60-second explainer, recorded frame by frame, with an original soundtrack. Tap the speaker icon for sound. The market row is the real close of Friday 9 October 2026 and the candles are real NIFTY 50 sessions; the usage meters show demo figures.

Overview

What the row shows

One row in the band above the prompt, under the two usage meters, on the daily timeframe. This is how it read at the close on Friday 9 October 2026.

close NIFTY 50 22,520.45 ▲1.30% CNX500 >200 DMA 39% A/D 2.17 NNH -27 daily · Fri 3:30pm
  • NIFTY 50: the last price and the change on the day, mint when up and coral when down.
  • CNX500 >200 DMA: the share of Nifty 500 stocks trading above their 200-day moving average.
  • A/D: Nifty 500 stocks that rose for every one that fell.
  • NNH: net new highs, the Nifty 500 stocks at a 52-week high minus those at a 52-week low.
  • market or close: in NSE hours (9:15 am to 3:30 pm IST, weekdays) the row reads market with the live time. After the close it reads close, keeps the last session's figures until the next open, and names the day.

One command away

The chart floats

Type /pulse and the NIFTY 50 daily chart opens in a small window over the terminal: sixty sessions as candlesticks, the 10-day and 21-day EMA as lines, a price grid and the last close. The keyboard stays with the terminal, and my next message closes the window.

It took three tries to get there. A picture drawn inside the terminal came out as its caption, because my terminal setup does not pass pictures through. So the first version drew the candles in coloured characters. Real candlesticks needed a real window, so I mocked up three ways on the same candles and picked the floating window.

Behind it

How it was made

Built with Claude Code, as a Claude Code mod: a small plugin that Claude Code loads into itself. It reads public market data about every five minutes in market hours, and every open session shares that one reading, so a dozen sessions cost the same as one. Weekends cost nothing. If a source fails, the row says so instead of going blank. Twenty-nine automated tests run against Claude Code's own engine, and it was proven in live sessions.

It is a personal build, not available to install. It shows figures only and never says what to do with them; nothing here is investment advice.
